## 25. Maps Intelligence & Advanced Local SEO

Extends §12 (Local SEO) with advanced geo-grid tracking, GBP audit, and review intelligence. Use when the user needs competitive local SEO analysis beyond basic NAP/schema checks.

### Geo-Grid Rank Tracking

Track local pack rankings across a geographic area, not just a single point.

#### Method
1. Define a grid around the business location (3×3 or 5×5 points)
2. For each grid point, check local pack rankings for target keywords
3. Map rank variations across the grid to identify weak zones

#### Grid Configuration

| Grid Size | Points | Best For |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| 3×3 | 9 | Single-location business, quick check |
| 5×5 | 25 | Multi-location, competitive market |
| 7×7 | 49 | Enterprise, dense metro area |

**Spacing:** Typically 1–3 miles between grid points depending on market density.

**Data source:** Requires DataForSEO SERP API with geo-targeting, or manual spot-checks using Google Maps with location simulation.

### GBP Completeness Audit

| Field | Weight | Check |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Primary category | Critical | Most specific subcategory selected? |
| Secondary categories | High | All relevant categories added (max 9)? |
| Business description | High | 750 chars, keywords natural, no keyword stuffing? |
| Hours | High | All hours set including special hours? |
| Photos | Medium | Min 10 photos, cover, logo, interior, exterior, team? |
| Products/Services | Medium | All services listed with descriptions? |
| Posts | Medium | Posted within last 7 days? Weekly cadence? |
| Q&A | Low | Owner answers to top questions? |
| Attributes | Low | All applicable attributes set? |

### Review Intelligence

| Metric | Benchmark | Action if Below |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Average rating | ≥ 4.3 stars | Investigate negative review themes; respond to all |
| Review count | ≥ 50 | Request reviews from satisfied customers (ethically) |
| Review recency | ≤ 30 days since last review | Activate review request workflow |
| Owner response rate | ≥ 80% | Respond to all reviews, especially negative |
| Review velocity | ≥ 2/month | Consistent cadence signals active business |

**Sentiment analysis:** Group negative reviews by theme (wait times, pricing, quality, service). Address the #1 theme operationally, not just in responses.

### Competitor Radius Mapping

1. Identify target keywords (e.g., "plumber near me", "dentist [city]")
2. Map competitors appearing in local pack within business service radius
3. Compare: GBP completeness, review count, review rating, NAP consistency, categories
4. Identify gaps: which competitors have attributes/photos/posts you're missing?

### NAP Consistency Audit

Check Name, Address, Phone consistency across:
1. Google Business Profile
2. Website (header/footer, contact page, schema)
3. Top directories: Yelp, BBB, Yellow Pages, Apple Maps, Bing Places, Facebook
4. Industry-specific directories

**Character-for-character** match required. Common issues:
- "St." vs "Street" vs "St"
- Suite/Ste/# variations
- Phone format: (555) 123-4567 vs 555-123-4567 vs 5551234567
