## 🔴 Critical Issues (fix immediately)

Finding: Health and dosing pages have no named author or medical reviewer
Evidence: article_seo.py found no author byline on 12 of 12 audited pages; /conditions/depression attributes content to "Chief Bear"
Impact: YMYL content without identifiable expertise is ranked conservatively and rarely cited by AI engines
Fix: Add a named author bio with credentials and a "medically reviewed by" line to /conditions/* and /dosing/*
Confidence: Confirmed | Severity: 🔴 Critical
First-Principle Observation: No page contains a Person author with a name, credentials or profile link
Dependency: Independent; unblocks F03 (Person schema)
Falsifiability: If comparable harm-reduction sites without named authors rank in the top 3 for the same queries, authorship is not the constraint
Leading Indicator: GSC impressions for /conditions/* queries within 6–10 weeks

## 🟠 High Priority (fix this week)

Finding: MedicalWebPage schema is used without verified medical credentials
Evidence: validate_schema.py reports MedicalWebPage on /conditions/depression with no reviewedBy or credentialed author
Impact: Authority-claiming schema without credentials risks a manual action for misleading structured data (§ 19 rule 8)
Fix: Replace MedicalWebPage with Article until a credentialed reviewer is named, then add reviewedBy
Confidence: Confirmed | Severity: 🟠 High
First-Principle Observation: The JSON-LD @type is MedicalWebPage and no reviewedBy property exists
Dependency: Blocked by the authorship finding for the reviewedBy step
Falsifiability: If Search Console shows no structured data issues and no manual action after 8 weeks, the risk is lower than assessed
Leading Indicator: Rich Results Test and GSC Enhancements report after deployment

## 🟡 Medium Priority (fix this month)

Finding: 123 strain pages receive a single internal link each
Evidence: internal_links.py found 123 URLs with exactly one inbound internal link
Impact: Link equity pools in hub pages; leaf pages are crawled less often
Fix: Add a "Related strains" block linking 3–5 sibling pages on each strain page
Confidence: Confirmed | Severity: 🟡 Medium
Falsifiability: If leaf pages already rank for their strain names, internal linking is not limiting them
Leading Indicator: internal_links.py shows 3+ inbound links per strain page on the next run
